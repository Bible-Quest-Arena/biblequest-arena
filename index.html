Questions

Good to know before you play.

Is it really free?

Yes. The Starter plan is free forever — all five modes, streaks, the Bronze league and the public leaderboard. Disciple+ removes the daily energy limit and adds premium rewards.

Which Bible translation does it use?

The New King James Version (NKJV) is the default, with the NIV available in settings and more translations planned.

What devices does it run on?

Any modern browser — phone, tablet, laptop. Add it to your home screen for a full-screen, app-like experience.

Can I use it with my church, youth group or school?

Yes — the Church / School plan adds team licences, group leaderboards and facilitator analytics, with live-room play on the way.

Is my data safe?

Accounts use server-side password hashing and secure, signed sessions. Only a display-safe profile appears on leaderboards, and only if you opt in.

Can I cancel anytime?

Anytime, in a couple of taps. Your progress, streak and library stay with your account.
